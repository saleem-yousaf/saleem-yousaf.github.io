A practical guide to STRIDE threat modelling

Threat modelling sounds abstract until you do it on something real. STRIDE gives you six lenses to hold up to a system, and most weaknesses reveal themselves the moment you ask the six questions in the right place. Learn the lenses below, then open any of the eight worked examples, click an element in its diagram, and see the threats and the fixes that element attracts.

How to read a threat model. Draw the system as elements and the flows between them, then mark the trust boundaries, the dashed lines where data crosses from less trusted to more trusted. At every element and every boundary, ask the six STRIDE questions. A threat is a plausible answer; a control is how you close it. The examples below are that method applied to six real designs.
